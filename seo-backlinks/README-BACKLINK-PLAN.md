# Backlink plan: Saad Car Rental (saadcarremtal.com)

## Sach pehle (important)
- Backlink kisi dusri website par banta hai, isliye zip ke code mein "lagaya" nahi ja sakta. Code mein sirf `sameAs` (profiles ko Google se jodna) ho sakta hai: wo `src/business.config.js` ke `profiles` aur `socialProfiles` mein ho gaya.
- "List of 175 Do Follow Bookmarking" wali list 2011 ki hai (PR0-PR6, Google PR ab khatam). Un par 150 sites par auto-submit karne se faida nahi, spam ka risk hai. Mat karo.
- Directory list 2013 ki hai: bohat si dead hain, aur zyada tar US/UK ke liye hain. Sirf neeche ki tracker mein di hui chunni hui sites use karo, aur submit karne se pehle check karo ke site chal rahi hai.
- Keywords wali file (document 3) backlinks ki list nahi hai, sirf search terms hain.
- Livprop wali sheet kisi aur business ke backlinks hain; usse sirf site names ka idea lo.

## Step by step
Week 1 (A: sab se zaroori): Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, Pinterest. Har jagah NAP-AND-BIO.txt se copy-paste karo.
Week 2 (B: directories): Yelp, Foursquare, Brownbook, Hotfrog, Tupalo, YelloYello, Pakistani directories, OLX. Roz 2-3 sites, ek hi din mein 20 nahi.
Week 3 (C): 1-2 helpful articles Medium/LinkedIn par, hotels/travel bloggers ko mention ke liye email.
Week 4 (D): local tourism sites se outreach.

Har listing ke baad URL tracker (backlink-tracker.csv) mein likho, aur real profile URLs `src/business.config.js` ke `profiles` array mein paste karo, phir build/deploy.

## Daily clicks 25-30 ke liye (backlinks ke ilawa)
1. Google Business Profile par hafte mein 2-3 posts, nayi photos, aur har customer se review lo. Local searches mein sab se bada asar yahi karta hai.
2. Search Console > Performance > Queries mein dekho kaun si queries par position 8-20 hai; un pages ka title/description behtar karo.
3. Har hafte 1 nayi guide/route page, jaise "Islamabad to Hunza with driver".
4. Domain ka spelling pehle final karo; agar badalna hai to backlinks banane se pehle badlo, warna sab links purane domain par ban jayenge.
